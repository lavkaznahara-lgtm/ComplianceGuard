Key Capabilities

  • Fuzzy Citation Verification: Checks that every cited quote really exists in the agent's log (hallucinated quotes earn no credit).
  • Epistemic Integrity Metrics: Measures how often the auditor is confidently wrong or incomplete, not just wrong.
  • Async Batch Pipeline: Retries, checkpoints, cost tracking, and strict budget caps.
  • Demo Mode: Streamlit dashboard with pre-baked simulated records, requiring no API key.

About the Project

ComplianceGuard is an independent auditor that analyzes AI agent behavior at the level of reasoning, actions, and evidence. It detects policy violations, data leaks, social engineering, and guardrail bypasses, preserving the "scars" of machine representation failures as actionable data.
